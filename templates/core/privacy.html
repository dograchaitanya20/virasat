Privacy & Archival Ethics

Last updated: November 2026

1. Community & Data Privacy

At Virasat, we respect your privacy. We do not sell, rent, or monetize personal user information. Information collected during account creation (username, email, bookmarked sites, quiz attempts) is strictly used to customize your learning journey.

2. Open Knowledge & Fair Attribution

All historical narratives, monuments, and cultural traditions documented on Virasat are curated for public education. Photographic media are drawn from Creative Commons / Wikimedia Commons / royalty-free archival licenses with full attribution.

3. Local Storage & Preferences

We use browser localStorage exclusively to persist your interface theme preference (Dark or Light Mode) and recent interactive map state.
